# Playbook: Creator Intel Pipeline

Know every creator in your niche better than they know themselves. This playbook builds a living database of creator profiles, content performance, and engagement — for brand deals, competitor analysis, or your own content strategy.

## What you get

A searchable database: creator handles, follower counts, posting cadence, average engagement, top formats, and trend participation — refreshed on schedule.

## Step 1 — Pick your actors

From the [catalog](../catalog/README.md):

- **TikTok profile & video scrapers** (296 actors) — profiles, video lists, stats
- **Instagram profile & post scrapers** (350 actors) — profiles, posts, timelines
- **Followers & engagement** — for audience-quality signals

## Step 2 — Build your creator list

Start with 50–100 creators in your niche: competitors, aspirational accounts, potential collaborators. A spreadsheet of handles/URLs is your seed list.

## Step 3 — Scrape profiles on schedule

Feed the handle list into your chosen profile scrapers. Run weekly — follower counts and engagement rates don't need daily precision, but trends do.

Capture per creator:

- Follower count + growth vs. last week
- Posts in last 30 days (cadence)
- Average likes/comments per post (engagement rate)
- Top 3 performing posts (format analysis)

## Step 4 — Score them

For brand deals, engagement rate beats follower count every time:

- **Engagement rate** = (avg likes + comments) / followers
- 1–3% is average, 5%+ is strong, 10%+ is exceptional
- **Watch for red flags**: 500K followers with 200 likes per post = bought audience

For competitor analysis, track format mix: what percentage of their top posts are talking-head vs. b-roll vs. trends? That's their playbook — now it's yours too.

## Step 5 — Use the intel

- **Brands**: vet creators before paying for sponsorships — real data kills influencer fraud
- **Creators**: reverse-engineer the top 10 accounts in your niche, then out-execute them
- **Agencies**: package "creator landscape reports" as a retainer service

## The math

One avoided bad sponsorship ($500–$5,000 down the drain on a fake-influencer post) pays for years of actor runs. The data is the cheapest insurance in creator marketing.

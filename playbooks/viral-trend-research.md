# Playbook: Viral Trend Research

Spot trends before they peak. This playbook builds a daily viral-intelligence feed from TikTok and Instagram — ranked by velocity, not vanity metrics.

## What you get

A daily scan of trending hashtags, sounds, and formats in your niche, with engagement velocity so you can tell what's rising vs. what's already dead.

## Step 1 — Pick your actors

From the [catalog](../catalog/README.md):

- **TikTok hashtags, trends & search** — 46 actors for hashtag and trend data
- **Instagram hashtags & explore** — 27 actors for IG-side trends
- **TikTok profile & video scrapers** — for pulling the actual top videos behind a trend

## Step 2 — Define your niche queries

Don't track "everything." Track 5–10 hashtags or keywords in your niche:

- Fitness: #gymtok #fitcheck #whatieatinaday
- Finance: #moneytok #sidehustle #investing101
- Your niche: the 10 hashtags your audience actually follows

## Step 3 — Schedule daily pulls

Run your hashtag actors on a daily schedule in the Apify Console. Each run captures the top videos, view counts, and engagement for that hashtag — your raw trend feed.

## Step 4 — Rank by velocity

The magic isn't the totals, it's the *change*. Compare today's numbers to yesterday's:

- **Views up 300%+ day-over-day** → rising trend, jump on it now
- **New hashtag appearing in top videos** → emerging format
- **Same videos topping for 5+ days** → saturated, skip it

A simple spreadsheet with yesterday vs. today columns does the job. Sort by growth rate, not total views.

## Step 5 — Act fast

Trend research is worthless without speed. When your scan flags a rising format:

1. Watch the top 5 videos in that trend
2. Note the hook, format, sound, and caption pattern
3. Film your version within 24 hours

Creators who ride trends in the first 48 hours get 10x the reach of week-two joiners.
